OPENSCAD COMMON PATTERNS — CONCISE SUMMARY
==========================================

FURNITURE (PROPORTIONS)
- Tables/chairs: tops with cube([...]); legs with cylinder(h,d) at corners (nested for with offsets).
- Chaise/sofa: body via stacked cube() sections; backrest using rotate([angle,0,0]) + cube().

FILLET/ROUNDED EDGES
- Global fillets: minkowski() of base solid with sphere(r=radius) (expensive).
- Rounded box corners: hull() of spheres at vertices (fast for boxes).

HOLE ARRAYS
- Double nested for with translate([col*spacing,row*spacing,0]) and cylinder(h,d).

COMMON TRANSFORMS
- Centering: translate([-w/2,-d/2,-h/2]) before children();
- Symmetry: union(){ children(); mirror([1,0,0]) children(); }.

BASIC VALIDATION
- assert(dimension>0) in validation modules.

COMMON FIXES
- 2D→3D: replace circle() in 3D context with cylinder(h=..., r=...).
- Use hull() with 3D children; add missing semicolons after every statement.
